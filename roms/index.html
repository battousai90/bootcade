The ROM button in the catalog is for Bootcade members. It is free: create an account, verify your email address, and download.

Open the catalog

Why an account?

The collection is served from a home server with limited bandwidth. An account lets us share it fairly: every player gets a quota, and nobody can take everything at once.

What counts

  • Each different ROM counts once, over a rolling 24 hours.
  • Downloading the same ROM again within those 24 hours is free: a resumed or retried download costs nothing.
  • A slot frees up 24 hours after each download.

Quota tiers

The more you play, the more you can download. The highest tier you reach applies.

How to climb

  • Play with the Bootcade launcher. On games with the Highscore badge, your score is read from the game itself and published under your account, nothing to type.
  • Take a world record. A first place counts once at least a few other players compete on that leaderboard.
  • Earn achievements. All 24 are listed on your profile with what each one needs. The 6 Prestige ones rest on contested leaderboards: 3 of them unlock Champion.

Play time is reported by the launcher, so it can lift you up to Regular but never to Champion: that tier rests on scores that the game itself confirms.
